Method: `prototype-kit/skills/analyze-reference.md`. Values read from computed styles in Chrome (approximate where stated).

**Read:** corporate / institutional consulting site. Flat, sharp-cornered, Helvetica-grade type, one hot red, warm paper beige. Static (no visible motion). The prototype must be *recognizably SBS but clearly better*: same palette DNA, far more craft.

**Extracted palette** (computed)

- Body text: `#555759`; muted `#77797A`; light grey `#C4C4C5`
- Brand red (CTA "Nous contacter", active nav, logo mark): `#E4042C`
- Paper beige (section bands, footer): `#F3F0EA`; deeper beige `#E7E1D4`
- Page: white `#FFFFFF`

**Extracted type**
- Site UI: Arial/Helvetica, bold headings (H1 48px/700, H2 36px/700), body 16px.
- Logo: high-contrast Didone serif wordmark "SBS" + condensed sans tagline, red horse/ribbon mark. Two PNG variants (dark, light/beige).

**Layout character**
- Centered ~875px column, large flat hero photo (Algiers waterfront), beige bands, **0px radius** everywhere, hairline white cards.

**Not copied:** Arial-only typography, the narrow centered column, the stock hero photo as a literal clone.
